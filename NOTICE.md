## What is copied from upstream, unchanged

Copied by `tools/port.py` from the commit above (paths kept):

- `content/assets/graveyard/`: block states, block and item models, item
  definitions and textures of the blocks the ported structures place; the
  entity textures of the ghoul (nine skins and eyes), revenant (and eyes) and
  nightmare; the lang files; the sound events of the three mobs and their
  `.ogg` files.
- `content/assets/minecraft/atlases/blocks.json`.
- `content/data/graveyard/structure/`: the structure templates (`.nbt`) of the
  ported structures.
- `content/data/graveyard/worldgen/structure_set/`, `worldgen/template_pool/`
  and `tags/worldgen/biome/` of the ported structures and mobs.
- `content/data/graveyard/recipe/`: the recipes of vanilla types whose items
  all exist in the port.

## What is changed (GPL-3.0 section 5a)

All changes were made in November 2026 for Horizon by the Saison 2 server.

- **Structures** (`worldgen/structure/`): upstream's structure type
  `graveyard:<name>` (Java class `TGJigsawStructure`) is rewritten as vanilla's
  `minecraft:jigsaw`. Its terrain and water check is written as Horizon's
  `horizon:start_checks`, its crypt and lich prison start heights as height
  providers. Spawn overrides naming mobs the port does not have are removed.
- **Processor lists**: upstream's three Java processors are no-ops in 4.0.0
  with the default config, so the lists are written empty.
- **Loot tables**: entries for items the port does not have, and the
  Chronicle pages (a Java loot condition), are removed.
- **Block, item, creative tab and entity type declarations**
  (`data/graveyard/horizon/`) are new: Horizon declares content as data on
  vanilla templates. Numbers are taken from upstream's `TGBlocks`, block
  classes and `create*Attributes`.
- **Natural spawns**: upstream's `graveyard:config_spawns` biome modifier is
  rewritten as NeoForge's `add_spawns` with upstream's default config values.
- **Convention biome tags** (`data/c/tags/worldgen/biome/`) are new.
- **Models and animations**: `mixins/graveyard/api/src/geo_data.rs` is
  generated by `tools/geckolib2rs.py` from upstream's GeckoLib
  `geckolib/models/{ghoul,revenant,nightmare}.geo.json` and
  `geckolib/animations/*/*.animation.json`: a translation into Rust tables.
- **Behaviour**: `mixins/graveyard/api/src/server.rs` and `client.rs`
  translate `GhoulEntity`, `RevenantEntity`, `NightmareEntity`,
  `AbstractGraveyardMeleeGoal`, the three melee goals, the renderers and
  `GraveyardEyesLayer` to Horizon's native mod API. See `README.md` for what
  differs from upstream.
- The tools in `tools/` and the rest of the native crate are new code.

No NeoForge source was read for this port.
